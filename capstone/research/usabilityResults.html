Usability Results

Three significant observations that emerged from testing:

  • Users were able to navigate through the questions just fine but wished that there was more interactivity between pages.
  • Users suggested that showing more data would create a bigger impact on them. Additionally they mentioned that there could be smoother transitions between the questions to help the flow become smoother.
  • Images used in the beginning could be swapped to be more connected to the theme. Suggestions were swapping the images to be friends smelling the flowers or just better lighting for the images all together.

Moving forwards...

I'm going to look into taking better pictures for the starting screen for this project. Additionally there will be transitions implemented between sceenes to support a better workflow and ease the users through the whole process. There's still a lot of work that needs to be done for this project but with that being said I'll keep user-interactivity close to my mind through this whole process.
