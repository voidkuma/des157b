Capstone Development – Proposal

My capstone project explores the effects of screen time through the lenses of Speculative Design and Challenging Mental Models, which we discussed in class. The project will take the form of an interactive webpage that asks users a series of questions about their screen time habits and relationship with technology.

The webpage will present prompts such as, “True or False: I spend around five hours on my phone each day,” along with additional questions about phone usage and commonly used applications. Based on the user’s responses, the environment of the webpage will gradually change. For example, users with lower screen time may see a background filled with nature and vibrant colors, while users with heavier device usage may experience a duller, less natural, and more unsettling atmosphere. This visual transformation is meant to encourage users to reflect on how technology affects their daily lives and mental state.

For development, I plan to use the TypeItJS library to animate the questions because I enjoyed working with it previously. I also want to incorporate another library to create smooth animations for elements such as growing flowers or environmental changes throughout the experience.

I am still exploring several additional ideas for the project. One possibility is beginning and ending the experience with the user looking into a mirror, where their reflection changes based on their responses and screen habits. Another idea is incorporating dynamic audio, where the music begins calm and gradually shifts in pitch or tone depending on the user’s answers. At this stage, I have many creative directions in mind, and I plan to continue refining which elements will best support the overall message of the project.

My target audience is primarily college students around my age, since they are the most accessible audience for research and are also among the most frequent users of technology and social media.
